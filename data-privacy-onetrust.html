OneTrust Consent & Preference Management

The OneTrust Consent and Preference (CPM) flow includes a consent web form, profile data storage, and a preference center where users can update and save their preferences.

  • Consent and Preference Web Form (Collection Point)
  • Data saved in consumer profiles on OneTrust
  • User can update and save their preferences using Preference Center
  • User wishes to update their preferences
